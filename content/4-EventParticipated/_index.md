During my internship, I participated in 3 events. Each event was a memorable experience providing new, interesting, and useful knowledge, along with wonderful gifts and unforgettable moments.

### [Event 1](4.1-Event1/)
&emsp;**Event Name:** AWS First Cloud AI Journey Community Day 2026

&emsp;**Time:** 09:00 - May 23, 2026

&emsp;**Location:** Ho Chi Minh City

&emsp;**Role in the event:** Attendee

&emsp;**Brief Description:** A community gathering that brought together Cloud and GenAI experts from major enterprises to explore real-world applications. Key topics focused on optimizing prompt context for LLMs, enhancing performance/security and reducing costs using Amazon CloudFront with Free Tier, the reality of non-deterministic LLM behavior, and building enterprise-grade multi-agent credit assessment systems.

### [Event 2](4.2-Event2/)
&emsp;**Event Name:** FCAJ Meetup 06/06/2026

&emsp;**Time:** 09:00 - June 06, 2026

&emsp;**Location:** Ho Chi Minh City

&emsp;**Role in the event:** Attendee

&emsp;**Brief Description:** A technical community meetup featuring practical system engineering case studies. Keynotes covered connecting Godot game clients with AWS WebSockets, core Docker concepts alongside layer caching mechanisms to optimize image build times, and leveraging AWS WAF with Machine Learning for proactive cyber attack defense. The meetup also featured an inspiring career journey session detailing the path from IT Helpdesk to Senior Sysadmin.

### [Event 3](4.3-Event3/)
&emsp;**Event Name:** Enterprise Cloud Architectures and Industry Applications at Amazon Web Services (AWS)

&emsp;**Time:** 09:00 - July 04, 2026

&emsp;**Location:** Swinburne University of Technology Campus

&emsp;**Role in the event:** Attendee

&emsp;**Brief Description:** A collaborative event with Swinburne students and industry experts from AWS, Cloud Kinetics, and Renova Cloud. The speakers provided critical insights into the modern "Senior-heavy" Cloud recruitment market, highlighted the gap between classroom data and production-level infrastructure, shared personal 5-year data engineering career journeys, and outlined essential mindsets for AI-Ready Freshers.
