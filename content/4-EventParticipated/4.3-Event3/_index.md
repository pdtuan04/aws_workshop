# Report on "Enterprise Cloud Architectures and Industry Applications at Amazon Web Services (AWS)"

### Event Purpose
Participating in the event to connect with Swinburne students, learn how businesses design and operate cloud computing solutions on AWS, listen to insights from experts about career roadmaps in the Cloud field, and understand the current difficulties that the market is facing.
### Speaker List
- NGUYEN GIA HUNG - Head of Solution Architect
- Khang Nguyen - Solution Architect, Cloud Kinetics
- NHU TRAN - AWS Account Manager
- BANH CAM VINH - Data Engineer, Renova Cloud
### Highlighted Content

#### Job Market & Career Trends
- He presented that current hiring trends of businesses demand quite high requirements.
- He shared that finding a job is better through networking with each other.
- The trend in the Cloud market is that there will be a lot of recruitment, but it will not be publicly posted but recruited internally.
- A lot of organizations and companies use Cloud.
- Survey on current salaries shows that there is no data for roles under 1 year, and current recruitment needs are almost non-existent.
- The current market is not patient with newcomers.
- The advantage of having certificates is only at the CV round.
- Talking about the pyramid recruitment model of businesses today, which tends to recruit more SENIORS.
#### School vs Industry "Same Skill, Different Game"

- Sharing about the differences between studying at school and working.
- Data in school is usually at an easy level, but practical data comes from many sources and changes constantly.
- Doing projects at school will be very different from doing them in reality.
- He presented stories about his 5-year journey working as a Data Engineer at Sieu Viet Group, Heineken Viet Nam, VNG ZaloPay, GreenSM, and Renova Cloud.
- In this industry, we have to communicate with many departments.
#### How to create Vietnamese Steve Jobs
- Talking about the topic of fears and whether we dare to speak out about those fears.  
- How to overcome fears to reflect on oneself and take more action.
- How to perceive fear correctly.
- Sharing about how to deal with the fear of disappointing parents.
- Small talk with students.
- The story of applying to AWS thanks to connection with others.
#### AI-Ready Freshers

- Sharing what needs to be prepared for the next stages.
- Must use AI the right way, must use it as much as possible but importantly, must understand it.
- Learning fundamental knowledge is very important.
- Sharing about the mindset "Going to school, you pay money to be allowed to make mistakes - Going to work, you are paid money to not make mistakes". 
- Always try to do more and go beyond, even if the score might not be higher, we still try to do it.
- Benefits when connecting with others and not focusing too much on the starting salary. 
- Evaluation aspects include 5 parts: Attitude, Competence, Experience, Exposure, Qualities.
- Proactively ask questions, create many relationships, and always go with a team.
- The more action taken, the luckier one gets.
### Key Takeaways
- Better understand the reality of the recruitment market in the Cloud sector and the skills that businesses prioritize.
- Realize the difference between classroom knowledge and actual requirements when working in a business.
- Know the importance of networking to expand career opportunities.
- Understand that Cloud certificates are only an advantage during the resume screening round, while actual capability and working skills are the deciding factors.
- Learn the mindset of using AI as a tool to support learning and working instead of relying completely on AI.
- Absorb many experiences regarding mindset, attitude, and self-orientation from speakers working in the industry.
### Professional Applications
- Continue to learn and practice AWS services through personal projects to improve practical deployment skills.
- Leverage AI to assist in researching documents, analyzing errors, and learning new technologies, while always verifying knowledge to understand the core nature.
- Proactively build personal projects and update them on GitHub to accumulate practical experience and make the personal profile stand out.
- Increase participation in technology meetups, workshops, and events to expand relationships with experts and the community.    

### Experience during the event

#### Learning from the speakers 
- The speakers not only shared specialized knowledge but also brought a lot of practical experience regarding the process of learning, working, and career development in the Cloud field.
- The sharing about the current recruitment market helps me better understand the skills needed to be prepared to meet the needs of businesses.
- The part sharing about AI and the learning mindset left a strong impression because it helped guide me to use AI more effectively during the learning process and project implementation.
- The networking session also created an opportunity for me to meet many students who have the same orientation to pursue the Cloud and DevOps fields.
#### Lessons learned
- Besides specialized knowledge, learning attitude, a proactive spirit, and communication skills are also very important factors in the career development process.
- Need to actively participate in practical projects to accumulate experience instead of just learning theory.
- Building a network of relationships in the technology community can bring many career opportunities in the future.
- Always maintain a learning spirit, stay ready to adapt to technological changes, and continuously improve oneself.
