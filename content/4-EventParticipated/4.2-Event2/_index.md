# Report on "FCAJ Meetup 06/06/2026"

### Event Purpose
Participating in the community meetup to listen to interesting presentations on diverse topics from other peers who have practical experience working in real-world systems, while learning from practical cases, new technology trends, and networking with other participants.
### Speaker List
- NGUYEN QUOC BAO - Swinburne University of Technology
- BAO HUYNH - Junior Cloud Native Developer - Endava Vietnam
- LE HOANG GIA DAI - Ho Chi Minh City University of Technology (HUTECH)
- VINH TRAN - System Administrator at Central Retail Group
### Highlighted Content

#### CONNECTING Godot Clients WITH AWS WebSockets
- He presented networking knowledge and network connections, helping to review common network architectures.
- He also introduced ENet in Godot.
- He demonstrated a small rock-paper-scissors game demo using several AWS services.
- He explained in detail how matchmaking works between two players and how disconnection is handled.
#### Docker - Virtualization Technology

- He presented the benefits of virtualization technology.
- Provided fundamental knowledge about Docker.
- He also talked about the Caching mechanism that helps speed up the building process.
- Listed some commonly used commands.
- Provided a few simple, detailed demos to help understand Docker better.

#### WAF & ML for Cyber Attack Detection
- Presented the combination of AWS WAF and Machine Learning to improve cyber attack detection capabilities.
- The solution helps provide proactive defense and automatically adapt to new threats.
- Explained the demo section.

#### Sharing his journey from IT Helpdesk to Senior Sysadmin position

- He shared that he did not come from a top-tier university, but thanks to his efforts starting from an IT Helpdesk position, he still advanced to the Senior Sysadmin role.
- Practicing by making many projects can be better.
- He talked about his interview sessions at various large companies.
- He shared some practical working stories and cases he experienced when working on different systems, such as hard drive failures causing downtime, and how to calmly handle the situation by step-by-step checking possible root causes and offering multiple solutions.
- The learning roadmap for Cloud.
### Key Takeaways
- Reviewed knowledge about network connections and how clients communicate with servers via WebSockets.
- Gained more understanding of Docker, especially the Docker Layer Caching mechanism which optimizes image build time.
- Learned how AWS WAF can be combined with Machine Learning to enhance detection capabilities and mitigate cyber attacks.
- Learned a lot of practical experience from professionals, especially how to handle incidents in production environments and the importance of staying calm during troubleshooting.
### Professional Applications
- Apply Docker knowledge to optimize the image building process through Docker Layer Caching.
- Use Docker Compose to deploy development environments quickly and consistently across machines.
- Better understand how to structure Dockerfiles and Docker commands to serve the deployment of applications in graduation thesis and personal projects.

### Experience during the event

#### Learning from the speakers 
- The speakers all shared with examples instead of just presenting theory, helping me better understand how technologies are applied in enterprises.
- The sharing about the career journey of Mr. Vinh left a strong impression because it showed that perseverance and a learning spirit are more important than the starting point.
#### Lessons learned
- Foundational knowledge is important, but practical experience and deploying real projects are what truly enhance skills.
- Need to be proactive in learning, exploring new technologies, and not hesitate to experiment with personal projects.
- When handling system incidents, it is crucial to stay calm and analyze the causes step-by-step instead of rushing to conclusions.
- Participating in meetups is a great opportunity to expand knowledge, update technology trends, and learn from experiences of people working in the industry.
