1. Go to the ElastiCache console, click **Create subnet group**, enter a name, and select your VPC.
![overview](../../../images/5-Workshop/5.4-elastic-cache/28.png)

2. In the subnet selection section, select **Private Subnet 5** and **Private Subnet 6**.
![overview](../../../images/5-Workshop/5.4-elastic-cache/29.png)

3. Click **Create** to successfully complete the subnet group creation for ElastiCache. 
![overview](../../../images/5-Workshop/5.4-elastic-cache/30.png)
